This year I designed and built a complete real estate website for a family business in Lagos (Francis Homes).  
It was one of the first projects where the work left my computer and started being used by real people.

### 01 // Problem I faced

The biggest challenge wasn’t the code.  
It was managing scope, communicating clearly with a non-technical client, and deciding what was actually necessary for a useful first version.

I also had to make sure the site stayed fast and clear on mobile, because most of the buyers the client deals with browse on their phones.

### 02 // What I learned

1. **Ship something solid first**  
   A clean, working site that the client can already share is more valuable than a feature-heavy version that stays in development.

2. **Mobile experience is not optional**  
   If the primary users are on phones, every decision should start from the mobile layout.

3. **Clear communication saves time**  
   Writing short updates and confirming decisions in simple language prevents a lot of rework.

4. **Client work teaches prioritisation**  
   You quickly learn the difference between “nice to have” and “this is blocking the client from using the site”.
