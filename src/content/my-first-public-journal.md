For the past 15 months I built in silence.

Today I decided to change that.



\### 01 // Problem I faced



I had projects, but almost no public proof of the thinking behind them.

Recruiters and other developers only saw finished demos, not the real decisions and mistakes.



\### 02 // What I learned



1\. \*\*Visibility matters\*\*

&#x20;  Shipping something public (even if small) creates accountability.



2\. \*\*Writing forces clarity\*\*

&#x20;  Trying to explain a problem in writing shows you where your understanding is still weak.



3\. \*\*Start simple\*\*

&#x20;  A clean Markdown-based journal is enough. I don’t need a complex CMS to begin.
